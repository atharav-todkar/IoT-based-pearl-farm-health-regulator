## 🔁 Comparison Study (IoT vs Manual System)

A 15-day comparative study was conducted between an IoT-enabled system and a manually operated pearl farming setup.

---

### 🔹 Performance Comparison

| Parameter     | IoT-Based System       | Manual System                |
| ------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| pH Stability  | Stable (7.35 – 7.52)   | Fluctuating (7.11 – 8.33)    |
| TDS Control   | Controlled (52–73 ppm) | Irregular variations         |
| Water Quality | Clear, low turbidity   | Algae growth observed        |
| Automation    | Fully automated        | Manual intervention required |
| Maintenance   | Low                    | High                         |
| Response Time | Fast (within minutes)  | Slow                         |
| Efficiency    | High                   | Low                          |

---

### 🔹 Key Insights

* IoT system maintained consistent and stable water conditions
* Manual system showed instability and environmental fluctuations
* Automated control reduced human effort significantly
* Improved water quality directly contributed to better pearl growth

---

### 🔹 Conclusion

The IoT-based system significantly outperforms traditional manual methods by providing stable conditions, reducing labor, and improving overall efficiency in pearl farming.
