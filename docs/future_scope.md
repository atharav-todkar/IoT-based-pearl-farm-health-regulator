## 🔮 Future Scope

The current system provides a strong foundation for smart pearl farming. Future enhancements can further improve automation, intelligence, and scalability:

---

### 🔹 Planned Improvements

* 🧠 **Machine Learning Integration**
  Predict optimal water conditions and automate decision-making

* ⚙️ **Automatic Dosing Pump**
  Precise control of pH using acid/base dosing

* 🌡️ **Water Heater Integration**
  Maintain optimal temperature during winter conditions

* 💧 **Advanced Filtration System**
  Improve water clarity and remove impurities

* 🐚 **Automated Feeding System**
  Schedule and control algae feeding for oysters

---

### 🔹 Scalability Enhancements

* Multi-tank monitoring system
* Centralized dashboard for large farms
* Mobile app for better accessibility

---

### 🔹 Vision

To transform traditional pearl farming into a fully automated, intelligent, and sustainable aquaculture system using IoT and AI technologies.
