## 📊 Results & Performance

The system was tested in a real pearl farming environment to evaluate its effectiveness in maintaining water quality and improving farming conditions.

---

### 🔹 Key Results

* Maintained stable pH range between **7.35 – 7.52**
* Manual system showed fluctuating pH between **7.11 – 8.33**
* Reduced manual monitoring effort by approximately **60%**
* Achieved faster response to environmental changes
* Maintained clear water with minimal turbidity

---

### 🔹 System Performance

* Real-time monitoring with continuous sensor updates
* Automatic response within minutes when thresholds exceeded
* Reliable communication using ESP-NOW protocol
* Efficient water regulation using solenoid valve system

---

### 🔹 Observations

* IoT-based system maintained consistent water quality
* Manual system showed algae growth and instability
* Automated control reduced human intervention
* Improved overall farming efficiency and sustainability
